Exactly what data we collect — and what we don't

Full transparency

✓ We DO collect

Contact details — name, email, company, position, phone, country.
Stream counts — total streams analysed, Very High / High / Medium / Low / Out of Scope counts.
Timestamp and country — when the analysis was run.

✗ We DO NOT collect

Stream numbers, names, or descriptions
Pressure, temperature, or composition data
Methane leak rates or calculated values
The HMB file — it never leaves your browser
Plant name, location, or project identifiers
We publish aggregated, anonymised statistics — e.g. "MethaneScan has helped 300 engineers across 22 countries identify an average 41% of streams as outside LDAR scope." Your contact details may be used to offer related professional services. You may opt out at any time.
